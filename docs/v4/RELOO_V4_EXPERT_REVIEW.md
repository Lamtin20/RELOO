## Disagreements and resolution

Premium immersion vs transaction clarity: no heavy3D across checkout; keep conditional map extrusion only. Removing all fallback UI vs truthful errors: retain neutral image-unavailable states, never replace a missing actual product image with generated inventory. Unified web/native component source vs platform conventions: share primitive values/contracts, retain Capacitor/native lifecycle/permission behavior. Mandatory GPS vs accessible service entry: owner requirement remains; real-device and legal/product review remain blockers, not an invented exception. Google preference vs unavailable API: keep explicit configured providers and provider attribution; do not silently claim Google was used.

Independent design, legal, security, business and platform approval is still required where the release process requires it. No rubric score or expert approval is fabricated.
